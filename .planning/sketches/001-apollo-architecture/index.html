Known Limitation (LFO rhythm): Calls can carry timbral rhythm via LFO/envelope modulation (e.g. a filter LFO creating a perceived pulse on a held note). The mel spectrogram gives the model perceptual access to this, but the response channel is MIDI-only — the model answers with note events, not synthesis parameters. Addressed in part by 999.1 (preset head) and 999.2 (synthesis-level rhythm response, deferred).
